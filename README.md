# PDF Signer ✍️

A dead-simple, browser-based PDF signing tool.

**[Click here to try it now](https://cainky.github.io/PDFSigner/)** (no download required)

## About Visual Signatures

This tool adds a **visual signature** to your PDF: your name rendered in a handwriting-style font, placed where you want it.

**This is valid for most everyday documents:**
- Rental agreements and leases
- School forms and permission slips
- Basic contracts and agreements
- Internal approvals and acknowledgments

This is the same type of signature used by DocuSign, HelloSign, and most e-signature tools.

**When you might need more:**
If you're signing high-stakes legal contracts, government documents, or working in regulated industries (banking, healthcare), you may need a cryptographic digital signature. Consult your legal counsel or compliance team for guidance.

- No installs.
- No runtime dependencies. Just open `index.html` and sign your PDF.

## Features

- Load any PDF.
- Type your signature (customizable fonts coming soon!).
- Place your signature wherever you want.
- Save the signed PDF.

## How to Use

### Option 1: Use Online (easiest)
Just visit **[cainky.github.io/PDFSigner](https://cainky.github.io/PDFSigner/)** (works in any modern browser).

### Option 2: Run Locally
1. Download `index.html`
2. Open it in your browser
3. Upload a PDF, type your signature, place it, and save!

Your PDF never leaves your device. All processing happens locally in your browser.
